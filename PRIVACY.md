# Privacy Policy for Glace APM
Effective Date: September 12, 2026

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
Glace APM is designed as an entirely local, offline password management application. No personal data, passwords, master passwords, or usernames are collected, transmitted, or stored on external servers by the developer, Vidyut Prabakaran, or any associated third parties. All credentials generated or saved within the application are encrypted and stored exclusively on your local device's storage drive.

## 2. Local Processing
All data processing occurs entirely on your device. Features such as the password strength checker and common password evaluation operate strictly within your local machine's memory. Glace APM does not send your passwords over the internet to check their strength, verify their security status, or communicate with external APIs.

## 3. Master Password Security
Your master password is the sole cryptographic key used to encrypt and decrypt your local credential vault. It is hashed locally and never transmitted, logged, or backed up externally. If you lose or forget your master password, your stored data cannot be recovered by the developer or any other entity.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Glace APM does not integrate with, sell data to, or transmit data to any third-party analytics, tracking, advertising, or cloud backup services.
